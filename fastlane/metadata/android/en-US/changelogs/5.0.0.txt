Message attachments: images are shown inline below the message and can be opened full screen, other files can be downloaded and opened with any app. Attachments are cached, so opening a message again does not download them twice, and every download shows its progress or the reason it failed.

New bottom navigation bar on the home screen: timetable, messages, free rooms and settings are one tap away.

Fixed: clicking a message sometimes did nothing until the app was restarted, inline images could stay stuck on a loading placeholder after switching between the Info Center tabs, and the back button of an open message went to the timetable instead of the message list.

This release no longer uses Google services and installs under a new package name (com.github.saimonn.betteruntis), so it can be installed alongside the official app. Uninstall older builds of this fork first: accounts and settings of the old installation are not carried over.
